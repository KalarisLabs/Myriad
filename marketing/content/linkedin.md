# LinkedIn launch post

We built MYRIAD at Kalaris Labs: a structured map of 10,000 atomic biotech agent tasks across 100 domains.

The project started with a simple problem. Agents are capable, but they repeatedly have to rediscover what good biotech work looks like: which decision comes first, what evidence is required, where uncertainty belongs, and when the correct answer is a no-call.

MYRIAD turns that problem into an operational graph. It spans genome assembly, multi-omics, protein engineering, drug discovery, genome editing, bioprocessing, clinical development, regulatory systems, and more.

A necessary clarification: these are 10,000 task-taxonomy nodes, not 10,000 magically validated laboratory protocols. The release is designed for routing, planning, retrieval, evaluation, and careful conversion into reviewed agent skills. It includes canonical and sanitized datasets, one Markdown record per node, validators, an NPX-compatible CLI, and three full seed skills.

Built by me, Srihari Muralikrishnan, with ChatGPT for Kalaris Labs.

The work now moves into Phase 3: converting selected nodes into evidence-backed, testable `SKILL.md` implementations.
