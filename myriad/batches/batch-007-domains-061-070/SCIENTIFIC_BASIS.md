# Scientific Basis and Scope Controls — Batch 007

## Operating principle

Batch 007 contains computational design, evidence synthesis, model evaluation, analytical planning, and decision-support nodes. It does not authorize autonomous wet-lab execution, clinical administration, patient-specific treatment decisions, enhanced-pathogen creation, or claims of regulatory compliance. Every task requires provenance, model or assay applicability, explicit uncertainty, and qualified-review flags.

## Evidence rules embedded in the taxonomy

- Binding, prediction, molecular structure, or sequence similarity does not establish efficacy, specificity, immunogenicity, or safety.
- Multispecific activity is interpreted through geometry, simultaneous engagement, receptor abundance, avidity, Fc biology, cytokines, and format-specific product quality.
- ADC conclusions preserve antibody, linker, payload, conjugation, DAR distribution, catabolites, and analyte-specific pharmacology.
- Degrader conclusions preserve binary engagement, ternary assembly, ubiquitination, target loss, functional response, proteome selectivity, exposure, and resistance as separable evidence layers.
- Vaccine and epitope conclusions distinguish processing, presentation, immune recognition, function, population coverage, protection, and clinical effectiveness.
- Immunogenicity conclusions distinguish predicted liability, product quality, binding ADA, neutralizing ADA, altered exposure, and clinical consequence.
- Engineered-cell conclusions preserve starting material, vector and editing integrity, process, identity, potency, expansion, persistence, safety, and long-term follow-up.
- Advanced-model conclusions are bounded by a declared context of use and require cell provenance, device or matrix characterization, measured exposure, functional validation, reproducibility, and transferability.
