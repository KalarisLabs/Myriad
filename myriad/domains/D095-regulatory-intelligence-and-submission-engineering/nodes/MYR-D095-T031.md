# Define decision scope and no-call criteria for evidence-gap, requirement, and traceability analysis in Regulatory Intelligence and Submission Engineering

## Objective

Create a versioned decision charter covering mapping of regulatory requirements and product claims to available, planned, missing, or contradictory evidence. Identify the accountable owner, intended users, permitted downstream actions, evidence thresholds, stopping conditions, and evidence that must be present before the task may return a decision rather than a no-call.

## When to route

Use when an agent must perform the bounded task 'Define decision scope and no-call criteria for evidence-gap, requirement, and traceability analysis in Regulatory Intelligence and Submission Engineering' within Regulatory Intelligence and Submission Engineering. Route only for computational analysis, data validation, evidence synthesis, or reporting; do not treat the node as experimental authorization, a clinical decision, a manufacturing disposition, or a regulatory determination.

## Completion evidence

- `machine_readable_result`
- `explicit_pass_warning_fail_or_no_call_status`
- `input_and_reference_provenance`
- `tool_model_parameter_and_version_provenance`

## No-call conditions

- required inputs or provenance are missing
- the method is outside its validated or documented applicability domain
- material evidence conflicts cannot be reconciled
- the requested action exceeds computational-advisory scope
