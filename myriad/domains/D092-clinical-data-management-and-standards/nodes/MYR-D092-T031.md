# Define decision scope and no-call criteria for external data transfer, ingestion, and reconciliation in Clinical Data Management and Standards

## Objective

Create a versioned decision charter covering controlled receipt and reconciliation of laboratory, imaging, eCOA, device, pharmacokinetic, biomarker, randomization, and vendor data. Identify the accountable owner, intended users, permitted downstream actions, evidence thresholds, stopping conditions, and evidence that must be present before the task may return a decision rather than a no-call.

## When to route

Use when an agent must perform the bounded task 'Define decision scope and no-call criteria for external data transfer, ingestion, and reconciliation in Clinical Data Management and Standards' within Clinical Data Management and Standards. Route only for computational analysis, data validation, evidence synthesis, or reporting; do not treat the node as experimental authorization, a clinical decision, a manufacturing disposition, or a regulatory determination.

## Completion evidence

- `machine_readable_result`
- `explicit_pass_warning_fail_or_no_call_status`
- `input_and_reference_provenance`
- `tool_model_parameter_and_version_provenance`

## No-call conditions

- required inputs or provenance are missing
- the method is outside its validated or documented applicability domain
- material evidence conflicts cannot be reconciled
- the requested action exceeds computational-advisory scope

Computational-advisory only. This taxonomy node is not a substitute for a task-specific validated skill, current primary references, local procedures, or qualified human authorization.
