# Sanitization policy

The sanitized distribution is generated from the canonical release and:

- preserves the original scientific task title and objective;
- normalizes encoding, whitespace, routing keywords, and field names;
- removes internal normalization provenance and legacy report details;
- adds explicit human-review and no-call controls;
- makes no new claims of empirical, clinical, manufacturing, or regulatory validation;
- excludes the full text of third-party articles and other redistributable-risk inputs.

Sanitization is not anonymization of clinical or proprietary datasets; no such datasets are bundled.
